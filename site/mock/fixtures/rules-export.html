Current Rules (Do Not Edit this page is reference)

Welcome to Black in a Flash, a dedicated space for Black gamers! While we appreciate and see multiple teams around the content creation space we don't see one that is just for us, and that is what this Discord hopes to alleviate: the creation of a space where we can authentically and openly be ourselves.

Familiarize yourselves with the rules before you join the discord.

Failure to comply with the rules may lead to moderator action.

  1. The moderation team reserve the right to remove anyone from the space.

If you cannot abide the rules or plainly speaking are not a good fit for the space, the moderators can remove you at will.

  1. Be respectful of others.

We will not tolerate any forms of harassment or bigotry, such as- but not limited to- harassment about race, gender/identity expression, sexual orientation, religion, disability, physical appearances. There's a line between a friendly roast and being a jerk.

  1. First and foremost, this space is to adapt, learn, and grow.

Let's try to keep that as the primary focus. It's okay to have off topic conversations or to be upset about things, but this is a space to empower ourselves. If you are going to detract from the experience of others, there may be moderator intervention.

You can head to the landing channel and type a message so you gain access to the rest of the discord. If you are unsure of something, you are welcome to ping the Aunties / Uncles role. 


Suggestions for Revamped Rules

Welcome to Black in a Flash, a dedicated space for Black gamers! While we appreciate and see multiple teams around the content creation space we don't see one that is just for us, and that is what this Discord hopes to alleviate: the creation of a space where we can authentically and openly be ourselves.

  1. First and foremost, we are community first.
  1. This space is to adapt, grow, and learn. We expect members to support and uplift each other. Any behavior that does not align with this value WILL NOT BE TOLERATED.
  1. Be respectful to others.
  1. We do not tolerate any form of harassment, bigotry, and hatred. There’s a fine line between a friendly joke/roast and being a jerk.
  2. Abuse or harassment of BaF Leads, moderation team, and other community volunteers WILL NOT BE TOLERATED
  1. Maintain the privacy and confidentiality of the community.
  1. Do not share member emails, server links, server surveys, or server content on public social media or websites.
  2. Do not share confidential info under embargo
  3. Do not share members’ personal info (aka no doxxing)
  1. Keep relevant conversations in the appropriate channels.
  1. Do not share a pic of what you ate for dinner in #off-topic. We have #recipes-and-food-pics
  1. Be mindful of voice chat behaviors.
  1. Do not continually and intentionally cause disruption of discussions.
  2. Keep content appropriate
  3. Respect moderator actions
  1. Moderators reserve the right to mute, deafen, disconnect or move members who break the rule
  1. The moderation team reserves the right to remove anyone from this space.

  1. If you cannot abide by the rules or, plainly speaking, are not a good fit for the space, the moderators can remove you at will

Banworthy Offenses

  • Theft of any kind
  • Harassment/Pestering
  • Any blatant -ism/bigotry
  • Threats of violence
  • Persistent blatant disrespect of leads and/or moderation team after a disciplinary action is taken
